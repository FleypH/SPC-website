Privacy Policy

Shield Point Capital (Private) Limited (“Shield Point Capital”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal information entrusted to us by users of the FinEra Inclusive Credit Marketplace (“FinEra”, “the Platform”, or “the Service”).

FinEra is a digital financial technology platform that connects borrowers seeking financing opportunities with licensed financial institutions and lenders. The Platform enables users to submit financing requests, communicate with lenders, exchange relevant documents, and access suitable financing opportunities.

1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose personal information when individuals use our Platform and related services.

By accessing or using FinEra, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the processing of your personal information as described herein.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Borrowers who use FinEra to seek financing opportunities;
  • Lenders and financial institutions that use FinEra to provide financing opportunities;
  • Employees, representatives, and authorised users of organisations using the Platform; and
  • Visitors and users interacting with our digital services.

2.Information We Collect

To provide, secure, and improve our services, Shield Point Capital may collect different categories of personal information depending on how you use FinEra.

2.1 Information Collected from Borrowers

Borrowers may provide the following information:

  • Full name and identification details;
  • National identification number or passport information;
  • Contact information, including phone number and email address;
  • Residential and postal address;
  • Employment information, including employer details and employment status;
  • Salary and income information;
  • Business information, where applying for business financing;
  • Financial information relevant to loan assessment;
  • Loan application details and financing requirements;
  • Supporting documents uploaded to the Platform;
  • Communication records between borrowers and lenders, including messages and shared documents.

2.2 Information Collected from Lenders

Financial institutions and lenders may provide:

  • Institution registration and business details;
  • Contact details of authorised representatives and employees;
  • Branch and operational information;
  • Lending products and financing offerings;
  • Licensing and regulatory documentation;
  • Documents uploaded for verification and compliance purposes.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When users access FinEra, we may automatically collect technical information, including:

  • Device information;
  • Browser type and version;
  • IP address;
  • Login activity and access history;
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies;
  • Session information;
  • Platform usage activity and security logs.

This information helps us maintain platform security, improve user experience, detect fraud, and ensure reliable service delivery.

3.Why We Collect and Use Personal Information

Shield Point Capital collects and processes personal information to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the FinEra Inclusive Credit Marketplace. We only process personal information for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

We collect and use personal information for the following purposes:

3.1 Know Your Customer (KYC) and Identity Verification

To verify the identity of borrowers, lenders, and authorised users, prevent impersonation, and ensure that users accessing the Platform are properly identified.

3.2 Fraud Prevention and Risk Management

To detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to fraudulent activities, unauthorised access, misuse of the Platform, and other security risks.

3.3 Loan Matching and Financing Opportunities

To analyse borrower financing requests, match borrowers with suitable lenders, and enable financial institutions to evaluate potential financing opportunities.

3.4 Communication and Document Exchange

To facilitate secure communication between borrowers and lenders, including messaging, sharing of relevant documents, and updates relating to financing applications.

3.5 Regulatory and Legal Compliance

To comply with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, reporting obligations, and requests from authorised regulatory authorities.

3.6 Customer Support and Service Delivery

To respond to user enquiries, provide technical assistance, resolve disputes, and improve the overall user experience.

3.7 Platform Analytics and Improvement

To analyse platform usage, monitor performance, identify areas for improvement, and develop better services and features.

3.8 Security and System Protection

To protect user information, maintain platform integrity, monitor suspicious activities, enforce security controls, and safeguard against cyber threats.

4.Data Sharing and Disclosure

Shield Point Capital respects the privacy of users and does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

We may share personal information only where necessary for providing FinEra services, complying with legal obligations, protecting users, or maintaining the security and functionality of the Platform.

Personal information may be shared with the following categories of recipients:

4.1 Licensed Financial Institutions and Lenders

Information may be shared with licensed banks, microfinance institutions, and other authorised financial service providers selected or matched through FinEra for the purpose of evaluating financing requests and providing loan opportunities.

4.2 Identity Verification and KYC Service Providers

We may share relevant information with authorised identity verification providers to verify user identities, prevent fraud, and support compliance requirements.

4.3 Regulatory Authorities

Personal information may be disclosed to regulatory bodies and government authorities where required by law, regulation, court order, or official request.

4.4 Law Enforcement Agencies

We may disclose personal information to law enforcement authorities where necessary to investigate fraud, illegal activities, security incidents, or where disclosure is legally required.

4.5 Professional Advisers

We may share information with authorised professional advisers, including legal, accounting, auditing, and compliance advisers, where necessary for business operations and regulatory compliance.

4.6 Service Providers and Data Processors

We may engage trusted third-party service providers that assist us with technology infrastructure, cloud hosting, cybersecurity, communications, analytics, and other operational services. Such providers are required to process information only for authorised purposes and maintain appropriate security safeguards.

Shield Point Capital does not sell users’ personal information to advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes unrelated to the operation of FinEra.

5.Data Retention

Shield Point Capital retains personal information only for as long as necessary to provide FinEra services, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain appropriate business records.

The period for which personal information is retained depends on factors including:

  • The purpose for which the information was collected;
  • Legal, regulatory, and compliance requirements;
  • Financial record-keeping obligations;
  • Security, fraud prevention, and audit requirements;
  • The need to maintain evidence of transactions and communications.

Where personal information is no longer required for legitimate business or legal purposes, Shield Point Capital will securely delete, anonymise, or dispose of such information in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

Certain information may be retained for longer periods where required by law, regulatory authorities, financial compliance obligations, or where necessary to protect the rights and interests of Shield Point Capital, users, and authorised partners.

Users may request information about the retention of their personal data or request deletion where permitted by applicable laws. However, deletion requests may be limited where retention is required for legal, regulatory, security, or legitimate business purposes.

6.Data Security

Shield Point Capital is committed to protecting personal information collected through the FinEra Inclusive Credit Marketplace. We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse of personal information.

Our security measures include:

6.1 Encryption and Data Protection

We use encryption and other security measures to protect sensitive information during transmission and storage, including personal, financial, and identity-related information.

6.2 Secure Infrastructure

User information is stored and processed using secure systems and infrastructure designed to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.

6.3 Access Controls

Access to personal information is restricted to authorised individuals who require such information to perform their responsibilities.

6.4 Role-Based Permissions

We apply role-based access controls to ensure that employees, administrators, and authorised users can only access information relevant to their roles.

6.5 Audit Logs and Monitoring

We maintain system logs and monitoring mechanisms to track access, detect suspicious activities, support investigations, and improve platform security.

6.6 Security Improvements

We regularly review and improve our security practices to address emerging risks, vulnerabilities, and changes in technology.

While we take reasonable measures to protect personal information, no digital system can guarantee complete security.

Users are encouraged to protect their account credentials and notify us of any suspected unauthorised access.

7.User Rights Regarding Personal Information

Shield Point Capital respects the rights of individuals regarding their personal information and provides users with control over how their information is processed in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Users may exercise the following rights:

7.1 Right to Access Information

Users have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about them and receive information about how their data is being processed.

7.2 Right to Correction

Users have the right to request correction or updating of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal information.

7.3 Right to Deletion

Users may request deletion of their personal information where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected. However, deletion requests may be limited where we are required to retain information for legal, regulatory, security, financial, or legitimate business purposes.

7.4 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, users have the right to withdraw their consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing conducted before consent was withdrawn.

7.5 Right to Request Copies of Personal Information

Users may request a copy of their personal information held by Shield Point Capital, subject to applicable verification and legal requirements.

7.6 Right to Lodge Complaints

Users have the right to raise concerns or complaints regarding the processing of their personal information. Users may contact Shield Point Capital directly, and where applicable, may lodge complaints with the relevant data protection authority.

To protect user privacy and security, we may require identity verification before processing requests relating to personal information.

8.Cookies and Similar Technologies

FinEra uses cookies and similar technologies to improve platform functionality, enhance security, analyse usage, and provide a better user experience.

Cookies are small data files stored on a user’s device when they access a website or digital platform. They help us recognise users, maintain secure sessions, and understand how the Platform is used.

We may use the following types of cookies:

8.1 Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of FinEra. They enable core functions such as user authentication, account access, secure sessions, and platform navigation.

8.2 Security Cookies

These cookies help protect user accounts and the Platform by supporting security measures such as fraud detection, authentication verification, and prevention of unauthorised access.

8.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with FinEra, monitor platform performance, identify improvements, and enhance user experience.

Users may manage or disable cookies through their browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and availability of some Platform features.

A fuller explanation of how we use cookies is available on our Cookies page.

9.International Transfer of Personal Information

Shield Point Capital primarily processes and stores personal information within Zimbabwe. However, in certain circumstances, personal information may be transferred, stored, or processed outside Zimbabwe where necessary for the operation of FinEra.

Such transfers may occur when we use trusted third-party service providers, technology infrastructure providers, cloud hosting services, security providers, or other authorised partners located in other jurisdictions.

Where personal information is transferred outside Zimbabwe, Shield Point Capital will ensure that such transfers are conducted only where legally permitted and that appropriate safeguards are implemented to protect personal information.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that third parties processing personal information maintain appropriate security and confidentiality standards consistent with applicable data protection requirements.

10.Zimbabwe Compliance

FinEra processes personal information in accordance with applicable Zimbabwean data protection laws, including the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07], together with any applicable regulations and lawful requirements.

Where FinEra expands into other jurisdictions, it will comply with the applicable privacy and data protection laws of those countries.
