Cookies

How FinEra uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the Platform secure, functional, and easy to use.

FinEra uses cookies and similar technologies to improve platform functionality, enhance security, analyse usage, and provide a better user experience.

This page explains what cookies are, the types of cookies we use, and the choices available to you. It expands on Section 8 of our Privacy Policy.

1.What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored on a user’s device when they access a website or digital platform. They help us recognise users, maintain secure sessions, and understand how the Platform is used.

Similar technologies — such as session storage and security tokens — may be used alongside cookies for the same purposes.

2.How We Use Cookies

Shield Point Capital uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in and maintain secure sessions;
  • Protect user accounts and detect fraudulent or unauthorised activity;
  • Remember your preferences, including your cookie choices;
  • Understand how the Platform is used and monitor performance;
  • Identify improvements and enhance the overall user experience.

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes unrelated to the operation of FinEra.

3.Types of Cookies We Use

We may use the following types of cookies:

8.1

Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of FinEra. They enable core functions such as user authentication, account access, secure sessions, and platform navigation.

8.2

Security Cookies

These cookies help protect user accounts and the Platform by supporting security measures such as fraud detection, authentication verification, and prevention of unauthorised access.

8.3

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with FinEra, monitor platform performance, identify improvements, and enhance user experience.

4.Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit our website, a cookie notice is displayed at the bottom of the screen. You can choose to accept or reject non-essential cookies from that notice, and your choice is remembered on your device.

Necessary and security cookies cannot be switched off, because the Platform cannot operate securely without them.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing the cookies stored by your browser for this website, after which the notice will be shown again on your next visit.

5.Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Users may manage or disable cookies through their browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies currently stored on your device;
  • Delete some or all stored cookies;
  • Block cookies from specific websites;
  • Block third-party cookies entirely;
  • Clear all cookies when you close the browser.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and availability of some Platform features, including secure sign-in.

6.Questions About Cookies

For more detail on how we handle personal information, please read our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.
