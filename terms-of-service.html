1.Introduction

FinEra provides a digital marketplace that connects individuals, businesses, and other borrowers seeking financing opportunities with licensed financial institutions and authorised lenders. The Platform enables users to submit financing requests, receive potential financing opportunities, communicate with lenders, exchange relevant documents, and manage financing-related interactions.

FinEra acts as a technology platform that facilitates connections between borrowers and lenders. FinEra does not directly provide loans, guarantee approval of financing applications, determine lending decisions, or act as a financial institution unless expressly stated otherwise.

Lenders using FinEra are responsible for making their own independent lending decisions, conducting assessments, and complying with applicable financial regulations.

These Terms are designed to ensure a secure, transparent, and fair environment for all users, including borrowers, lenders, and authorised representatives.

3.About Shield Point Capital

Shield Point Capital (Private) Limited is a financial technology company focused on improving access to financial opportunities through innovative digital solutions.

The Company operates the FinEra Inclusive Loan Marketplace, a technology platform designed to connect borrowers, including individuals, businesses, and organisations, with licensed financial institutions and authorised lenders.

Through FinEra, Shield Point Capital provides digital infrastructure that enables users to submit financing requests, discover suitable financing opportunities, communicate securely with lenders, exchange relevant documentation, and track financing application processes.

Shield Point Capital does not act as a bank, microfinance institution, or direct lender unless expressly authorised by applicable regulatory authorities. The Company does not guarantee loan approval, financing availability, interest rates, or lending decisions. All lending decisions are made independently by participating financial institutions and lenders based on their own assessment criteria, policies, and regulatory obligations.

The Company’s role is to provide a secure, transparent, and efficient technology platform that supports financial inclusion and improves access to financing opportunities.

4.About FinEra

FinEra Inclusive Loan Marketplace (“FinEra”) is a digital financial technology platform operated by Shield Point Capital (Private) Limited that connects borrowers with licensed financial institutions and authorised lenders.

FinEra is designed to simplify access to financing by allowing users to submit a single financing request and explore multiple potential financing opportunities from participating lenders. The Platform supports various financing needs, including business financing, salary-based loans, education financing, and other approved financing solutions offered by registered lenders.

Through FinEra, borrowers can create profiles, submit financing requirements, upload supporting documents, communicate with lenders, receive financing offers, and track the progress of their applications.

For lenders, FinEra provides digital tools to discover potential borrowers, review financing requests, communicate securely, manage applications, and improve efficiency in the lending process.

FinEra operates as a technology marketplace and does not independently approve, reject, issue, or guarantee loans. All financing decisions, loan terms, interest rates, repayment schedules, and contractual relationships between borrowers and lenders are determined solely by the relevant lender.

The purpose of FinEra is to promote financial inclusion by creating a more accessible, transparent, and efficient connection between people seeking financing and institutions providing financing opportunities.
